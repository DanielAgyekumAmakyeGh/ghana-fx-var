\section*{Purpose}

This memo requests review by the Model Validation Committee of the
FX VaR framework developed for the USD/GHS book. The framework comprises
four engines:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Parametric VaR (variance-covariance, normal assumption)
  \item Historical simulation VaR (empirical distribution)
  \item GARCH(1,1) conditional VaR (Student-$t$ errors)
  \item Daily FX risk report (historical simulation as primary)
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Findings}

\subsection*{F1 --- Non-normality is definitive}

Kurtosis of 147.78 (normal = 0) rejects the normal assumption at any
realistic significance level. Skewness of $-1.84$ indicates asymmetric
tail risk.

\subsection*{F2 --- Parametric VaR fails Kupiec}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}lX@{}}
\toprule
Observations (rolling 250-day) & 2{,}475 \\
Expected breaches (1\%) & 24.75 \\
Observed breaches & \textbf{60} \\
$LR$ statistic & \textbf{36.27} \\
Critical value ($\chi^2_{1,0.95}$) & 3.84 \\
Verdict & \textbf{REJECTED} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsection*{F3 --- Expected Shortfall divergence}

Historical ES is $2.14\times$ the parametric ES on the same sample.
The parametric engine materially understates tail loss.

\subsection*{F4 --- GARCH confirms IGARCH regime}

$\alpha_1 + \beta_1 = 1.000$ indicates integrated GARCH: volatility
shocks to the cedi do not decay. GARCH conditional VaR is $2.32\times$
the full-sample parametric VaR.

\subsection*{F5 --- Two historical VaR windows, both reported}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}lX@{}}
\toprule
Full-sample VaR (2{,}725 days) & GH₵3{,}951{,}268 \\
Rolling 250-day VaR & GH₵9{,}581{,}018 \\
Recency uplift & 2.43\(\times\) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

Both numbers are correct and both are always shown together. The
full-sample figure is the long-run, unconditional estimate used for
capital planning; the rolling 250-day figure is the recent conditional
estimate used for daily limit monitoring. The framework never presents
either number in isolation.

\section*{Request}

The Committee is asked to:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Endorse historical simulation as the primary daily VaR measure.
  \item Require Expected Shortfall reporting alongside VaR.
  \item Approve the GARCH(1,1) model as a secondary conditional measure.
  \item Note the parametric engine's rejection for internal use.
  \item Approve the daily risk report format for Treasury and ALCO.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Limitations}
